Filtering for Regions with ≥ 3 Years

To estimate within-region changes over time, I need repeated observations per region. I need several years. With only 1 or 2 years per region, any estimated slope would be unstable; small noise would completely derail it. But I don't have that many regions with 4+ years (though I could re-run the analysis like that! As robustness check?). This first analysis therefore focuses on regions with at least 3 survey years, for as many observations as possible, but still 3+ years.
