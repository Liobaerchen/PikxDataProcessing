Living Well? A Global Data Analysis of Heat and Domestic Violence
Main Phase 1 Data Availability Analysis Exploring Moderators

Exploring Moderators

Exploring empowerment, urban heat island effects, wealth, cooling (electricity), and age gaps as moderators.

  • Assumption checks for moderators
  • Results and interpretation for moderators

Reminder: Research Question

Main Question

Are hotter-than-usual years within a region associated with increased prevalence of domestic violence?

Reminder: Model

DV ~ heat + rain + region FE + year FE

About the Empowerment Score

I coded empowerment as a levelled composite score.

overall empowerment = mean( autonomy, education, information access, economic empowerment )

All these variables were weighted equally, but autonomy, education, etc. were themselves composite scores. I did this so that the domain that was simply measured with more questions wouldn't determine the result, but to have them all have equal impact. Some variables were measured on different scales (e.g. years of education). I transformed those into percentages of the max value in the data.

Education

  • female literacy in % per region
  • Mean years of education

Information Access

  • "Women using the internet at least one a week (%)"
  • Women watching the news at least one a week (%)
  • Women listening to the radio at least one a week (%)

Economic Empowerment

  • "Women currently married or in union who were paid cash for their work, who decide alone how their money earned is spent (%)"
  • "Women currently married or in union who were paid cash for their work, earning more or equal than her partner (%)"
  • "Women who women who worked in the past 12 months who are paid in their work (%)"
  • "Women owning a house alone or jointly (%)"
  • "Women owning land alone or jointly (%)"

Autonomy

  • 'DP_decide_visits_p'

Moderation Analyses

Moderator: Empowerment

Hypothesis: empowerment buffers the effect of extreme heat on domestic violence.

dv ~ heat * empowerment + rain + region FE + year FE

Assumption Check 1: Within-region Variance

Mean within-region SD: 3.7639

Moderators with little within-region variation are difficult to identify in fixed-effects models because the model relies on within-region change over time. This is in percent! So just 3%!!

Assumption Check 2: Correlation with Heat

Correlation with heat: r = -0.1500

Looks good

Assumption Check 3: Multicollinearity

Moderator VIF: 1.0230

VIF looks good, too.

Results

β = 0.1237
SE = 0.2463
p = 0.6155

Interpretation

The effect was not big and not statistically significant... :(

This suggests the estimated heat effect appears relatively stable across observed levels of empowerment.

Moderator: Cooling Access

Hypothesis: access to electricity and cooling infrastructure buffers heat-related stress effects.

dv ~ heat * electricity + rain + region FE + year FE

Assumption Check 1: Within-region Variance

Mean within-region SD: 8.9884

Again, this is just percent... 8%. Small.

Assumption Check 2: Correlation with Heat

Correlation with heat: r = -0.0836

Looks good

Assumption Check 3: Multicollinearity

Moderator VIF: 1.0072

Looks good

Results

β = 0.0061
SE = 0.0864
p = 0.9441

Interpretation

No statistically significant moderation effect, and tiny beta.

Moderator: Mean Age Gap

Hypothesis: larger age gaps could increase vulnerability and therefore intensify the heat effect.

dv ~ heat * age_gap_mean + rain + region FE + year FE

Assumption Check 1: Within-region Variance

Mean within-region SD: 0.3114

This is VERY low. Basically almost no within-region variation.

Assumption Check 2: Correlation with Heat

Correlation with heat: r = 0.2467

Not alarming, but definitely higher than the others.

Assumption Check 3: Multicollinearity

Moderator VIF: 1.0863

Still looks fine.

Results

β = -1.0032
SE = 2.0936
p = 0.6318

Interpretation

No significant moderation effect.

Again basically no within-region variation, hard to estimate.

Moderator: Wealth

Hypothesis: wealthier regions may have more resources to buffer heat-related stress.

dv ~ heat * wealth + rain + region FE + year FE

Assumption Check 1: Within-region Variance

Mean within-region SD: 5.5998

Better than empowerment.

Assumption Check 2: Correlation with Heat

Correlation with heat: r = -0.0158

Basically zero.

Assumption Check 3: Multicollinearity

Moderator VIF: 1.0003

Extremely clean.

Results

β = 0.0597
SE = 0.1365
p = 0.6618

Interpretation

Tiny and non-significant.

Moderator: Urbanicity

Hypothesis: urban heat island effects may intensify heat exposure.

dv ~ heat * urbanicity + rain + region FE + year FE

Assumption Check 1: Within-region Variance

Mean within-region SD: 4.6417

Not huge, but workable.

Assumption Check 2: Correlation with Heat

Correlation with heat: r = -0.0632

Looks good.

Assumption Check 3: Multicollinearity

Moderator VIF: 1.0171

No issue.

Results

β = 0.0238
SE = 0.1329
p = 0.8581

Interpretation

Again almost no moderation effect.
